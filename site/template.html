Domicule tilings cover a board with pieces that join two cells sharing a side or only a corner; they are the perfect matchings of the king's graph. Its boards with both sides at least 3 have no Pfaffian orientation, so Kasteleyn's method does not count them. The entropy per site is s = __RESEARCH_S__ ± __RESEARCH_DS__, an estimate extrapolated from transfer matrices on cylinders of circumference up to 25, and is proved to lie between __RESEARCH_LOWER__ and __RESEARCH_UPPER__. The research page summarises the paper and its companion note.

How many tilings does a 2n × 2n board have? With dominoes, which join cells that share a side, the count is OEIS A004003 (a chessboard has __CHESSBOARD_DOMINOES__); with domicules it is OEIS A239273 (__CHESSBOARD_DOMICULES__). Both are counted here exactly up to 20 × 20, together with tilings of cylinders and tori and the domicule tilings sorted by their number of diagonal pieces. The gallery below numbers every tiling of a board, cylinder or torus and draws any single one; larger boards are sampled. The repository's contributions to the OEIS are listed in oeis/.

Solvers

Dominoes (A004003)

BoardTilingsNumberBigIntFormulaRuns

Number keeps the count in a JavaScript number and stops at 10×10, beyond which the count exceeds 253. BigInt counts exactly. Formula evaluates the product formula of Kasteleyn and Fisher as an exact resultant. Re-run with pnpm benchmark.

Domicules (A239273)

BoardTilingsBigIntModularRuns

BigInt counts exactly. Modular counts modulo primes below 231 in parallel worker threads, rebuilds the count with the Chinese remainder theorem and checks it against one more prime. Re-run with pnpm benchmark:domicule.

Domicules on cylinders

BoardTilingsBigIntModularRuns

node <solver> -r R -c C for a cylinder of circumference C. BigInt multiplies by the row transfer matrix. Modular goes cell by cell modulo primes below 231. The same two solvers count the tori. Re-run with pnpm benchmark:periodic.

Domicules on tori (A401670)

BoardTilingsBigIntModularRuns

node <solver> -r R -c C --torus for a torus. BigInt multiplies by the row transfer matrix and runs the torus once for every set of first-row cells covered from the last row. Modular goes cell by cell modulo primes below 231 and runs one set per rotation class. The same two solvers count the cylinders. Re-run with pnpm benchmark:periodic.

Diagonal pieces (A401673)

BoardTilingsBigIntModularRuns

Both solvers run the domicule transfer with a polynomial in the number of diagonal pieces in every state. Modular keeps only the coefficients of the parity a state fixes, counts modulo primes below 231 and rebuilds every coefficient with the Chinese remainder theorem. Re-run with pnpm benchmark:diagonal.
