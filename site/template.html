This is the site of Lattice Dimers, a research project that counts the ways to cover a board with pieces of two cells. A domino covers two cells sharing a side, a domicule two cells sharing a side or only a corner. A chessboard has __CHESSBOARD_DOMINOES__ domino tilings (OEIS A004003) and __CHESSBOARD_DOMICULES__ domicule tilings (OEIS A239273). The code, the data and the OEIS entries of the project are in the repository.

  • Gallery: numbers the tilings of a rectangle, a cylinder or a torus and draws any one of them; boards too large to number are sampled.
  • Benchmarks: the exact counts and the running times of the solvers in Rust, Python and JavaScript, on boards up to 20 × 20.
  • Notebooks, in the menu: the methods for dominoes, domicules and the triangular lattice, with code that runs whenever the site is built.
  • Research: the entropy per site of domicule tilings, estimated at s = __RESEARCH_S__ ± __RESEARCH_DS__ and proved to lie between __RESEARCH_LOWER__ and __RESEARCH_UPPER__, with the paper and its companion note.
