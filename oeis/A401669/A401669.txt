%N A401669 Decimal expansion of the entropy per site of the dimer coverings (perfect matchings) of the king's graph (square lattice with both diagonals), i.e., of domicule tilings.
%C A401669 In Ryłko (2026), the digits come from row transfer matrices on cylinders of circumference w <= 25, split by the parity of the number of dimers crossing a loop around the cylinder, and extrapolated in w. This gives s = 0.5655119110413253 +- 6*10^-16. The error bar is supported by several extrapolation models with backtests and by an extended-precision check of the rounding, but it is not a proof.
%C A401669 Rigorous bounds are 0.545343749727 <= s <= 0.582767477484 (Ryłko (2026)). They come from computer-assisted Collatz-Wielandt certificates on free strips of width 24, and the upper bound also weights the runs of cells whose dimers cross a cut.
%C A401669 The entropy per dimer is 2*s = 1.1310238220826... and the growth constant per site is exp(s) = 1.76034869404197....
%C A401669 For h*w even, the h X w king's graph has a Pfaffian orientation if and only if min(h,w) <= 2 (Ryłko (2026)), so Kasteleyn's method, which gives Catalan/Pi (A143233) for dominoes, does not apply.

%F A401669 s = lim_{h,w->oo} log(A239264(h,w))/(h*w) = lim_{n->oo} log(A239273(n))/(4*n^2).
