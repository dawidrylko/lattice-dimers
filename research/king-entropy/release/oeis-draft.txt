%N A000000 Decimal expansion of the entropy per site of the dimer coverings (perfect matchings) of the king's graph (square lattice with both diagonals), i.e., of domicule tilings.
%C A000000 Equals lim_{h,w->oo} log(A239264(h,w))/(h*w), and lim_{n->oo} log(A239273(n))/(4*n^2).
%C A000000 The terms were obtained from row transfer matrices on cylinders of circumference w <= 25, split by a conserved parity, extrapolated in w: s = 0.5655119110413253 +- 6*10^-16. The error bar is supported by several extrapolation models with backtests and by an extended-precision check of the rounding, but it is not a proof; only the 14 digits after the decimal point shared by s - 6*10^-16 and s + 6*10^-16 are listed.
%C A000000 Rigorous bounds: 0.545343749727 <= s <= 0.582767477484 (computer-assisted Collatz-Wielandt certificates on free strips of width 24; the upper bound uses a bound for the matchings across a cut that weights the runs of crossing cells).
%C A000000 The entropy per dimer is 2*s = 1.1310238220826... (13 digits after the decimal point) and the growth constant per site is exp(s) = 1.76034869404197... (14 digits after the decimal point); in both, only the digits shared by the whole interval s - 6*10^-16 .. s + 6*10^-16 are listed.
%C A000000 For h*w even, the h X w king's graph has a Pfaffian orientation if and only if min(h,w) <= 2, so Kasteleyn's method does not apply to the larger boards. For the domino case (square lattice) the corresponding constant is Catalan/Pi.
%C A000000 The surface free energy of a free edge is sigma = 0.242017935766251 +- 9*10^-15, so log(A239264(h,w)) = s*h*w - sigma*(2*h + 2*w) + o(h + w) is expected.
%C A000000 Numerically, with the corner free energy kappa = 0.36319625605 +- 3*10^-10, A239273(n) ~ C * d^(n^2) / f^n with C = exp(kappa) = 1.43791803..., d = exp(4*s) = 9.6027320273133..., f = exp(8*sigma) = 6.93196613224... (only the digits shared by the error bars of kappa, s and sigma); the relative error of the approximation is -2.0*10^-9 at n = 12.
%F A000000 s = lim_{w->oo} log(lambda_w)/w, where lambda_w is the growth rate of the number of domicule tilings of a strip of width w (lambda_w^2 = lim_{j->oo} A239264(2*j,w)^(1/j)).
